  1. Open the EMR in Chrome. On the test platform the address is http://localhost:18200/openmrs/spa.
  2. Log in with the account you were given and choose your location. The screens in this manual used the location Main Pharmacy.

A. Form Studio

Part A: for form builders

A1. What Form Studio is

Form Studio turns a clinical form (on paper, in Word, in Excel or in Bahmni) into a form the EthioEMR can show to clinicians, and checks it before anyone uses it.

  1. Start a form: from scratch, from a Bahmni export, or from the Excel template.
  2. Edit it on screen. You see the form as clinicians will, with pencil and + buttons.
  3. Link concepts. Every question is tied to a dictionary entry, so the answer is saved with a meaning the whole EMR understands.
  4. Add rules, for example "show this question only when Smoker is Yes".
  5. Check. Form Studio lists what must be fixed and what is worth a look.
  6. Export. You download a zip of files and a reviewer adds them to the content repository. Nothing goes to a live EMR from Form Studio.
WordMeaning
ConceptA dictionary entry with a fixed meaning, such as "Pulse" or "Yes". Every question and every answer points at one, so data from all forms and facilities can be counted together.
Visit DetailsThe standard first page of every EthioEMR form (visit date, provider and other standard fields). It is always there and cannot be removed or changed.
IdentityA form's name, uuid (a long unique code), encounter type and version. For a form that already ships it is locked, because facilities' saved data depends on it.
Encounter typeThe kind of visit the form belongs to (for example Consultation, Triage or Vitals). Only the project's existing types are offered.
Page, section, questionA form has pages, a page has sections, and a section has questions.
Repeating groupQuestions that can be filled in more than once, such as each medicine with its dose.
Content repositoryThe shared, reviewed folder of forms and concepts that every facility's EMR is built from.

A2. Open Form Studio and find a form

  1. Open the grid menu at the top right and choose Form Studio, or go to /openmrs/spa/form-studio.
  2. On the left, Create a new form offers three starting points: From scratch, From a Bahmni export and From an Excel template.
  3. On the right, Forms lists every form on this EMR. Type part of a name in Search this list, or change Filter by (Active is the default; All, Published, Unpublished, Retired).
  4. On a row, the pencil (Edit schema) opens the form in the editor and the arrow (Download schema) saves its file.

When you open an existing form its identity stays locked: you can change what is in it, but not its name, uuid or encounter type.

A3. Start a form from scratch

Choose From scratch. The window New form: the basics opens.

FieldWhat to write
Form nameWhat the form is called in the EMR, for example "Cardiac History Sheet". It cannot be the name of another form.
DescriptionWhat the form records, for whom and when. Aim for 18 to 40 words; a counter shows how many you have.
Encounter typeChoose from the list. Only the project's encounter types are offered.
VersionLeave it as it is unless told otherwise.
UUIDMade from the name for you. You do not type it.

Press Create form. The editor opens with the Visit Details page and a starter page, Vital Signs & Assessment, to build on or rename. If you already have the form as JSON (the text file a form is stored as), press Skip: I will paste a JSON and paste it into the Schema editor.

A4. Convert a Bahmni export

Use this when the form already exists in Bahmni, the older system.

  1. In Bahmni, export the form. You get a .zip file.
  2. In Form Studio choose From a Bahmni export and drag the zip onto the page, or click to choose it.
  3. Form Studio shows Matching concepts on this server with a counter while it looks up each question in this EMR's dictionary.
  4. Read What came out: the counts (pages, sections, questions, concepts matched, concepts to pick), then Needs a decision in the editor, For information, Not built (from EMR) and Rows that could not be used, each with the reason.
  5. Check the form name, description and encounter type at the top. If this rebuilds a form that already ships, Form Studio says Rebuilds "<name>" and keeps that form's uuid, so facilities' data stays linked.
  6. Press Open in the editor.
In BahmniIn the EthioEMR form

A set that can be added more than once ("allowAddMore")A repeating group.
Required, multi-select, starredCarried across with the same meaning.

A5. Use the Excel template

Most new forms start on paper or in Word. Copy them into the Excel template, one row per question, and Form Studio builds the form.

  1. Choose From an Excel template and press Download the empty template. Download a filled example (Cardiac History Sheet) gives one to copy from.
  2. Fill in the About sheet once: form name, encounter type and description. Fill in "Replaces" only when you rebuild a form that already ships.
  3. Fill in the Questions sheet, one row per question. Red headers must be filled in; blue ones are optional.
  4. Upload the file on the same page, then continue as in A4 from step 3. The review screen is the same.

Write the Question ID and the option exactly as in their rows. All 17 Word forms of the St. Petros batch (1,321 questions) fit the template with no lost rows.

A6. Edit the form on screen

The editor has two halves. On the left is your working view, switched at the top between Preview & edit, Rules and Schema editor. On the right are the live preview (what the clinician will see) and the Form Studio panel with the identity, the Check and the downloads.

ButtonWhat it does
Pencil (edit page, section or question)Change the label, type, options, concept, or whether it is required.
Bin (delete)Remove it.
Add page, Add section or Add questionAdd a new one in that place.
Add repeating groupAdd a group of questions that can be filled in several times, like medicines.
Add a question to this groupAdd one more question inside a group.
Rules buttonOpens the rules for that question (A8).

A red Concept not mapped tag means the question is not linked to a dictionary concept yet (A7). The Rules view lists every rule of the form as a sentence, for example Shown only when "Smoker" is "Yes". The Schema editor shows the form as JSON text, for experienced users. For forms that already ship, Visit Details and the identity cannot be changed.

A7. Concepts: pick or create

Every question and every answer needs a concept. Correct concepts are what let reports count the same thing across forms and facilities.

The concept dictionary

Open the concept dictionary (start page) reads every concept from this EMR's database: names, synonyms and other names, class, datatype, and how many shipped forms use each. The first time it takes about 25 minutes, with a progress bar; you can keep working, and a reload carries on where it stopped. After that it opens at once. Refresh reads the database again. The question dialog's concept search uses the same copy once it is ready.

Pick an existing concept (always first)

  1. Open the question's pencil and search by the question's name.
  2. Results are ranked. Concepts already used in other forms come first, marked used in N forms. Concepts that do not fit are marked with the reason, for example a drug used as a question, or a name that is too general ("NA", "Other").
  3. Choose the one that means exactly what the question asks.

Create a new concept, only when nothing fits

In the right-hand panel, Create new concepts (N unmatched) lists the questions and answers that still have no concept, with a proposal for each:

KindMade as
An answer optionFinding
A Yes/No questionQuestion with Yes/No answers (the shared Yes and No concepts)
A measurementQuestion, Numeric, with units, lowest/highest possible and decimals allowed
Other questionsQuestion, of the matching type

Check each name (Form Studio warns if it already exists), then press Create N concepts. The concepts go into the export files, not to a server. Never create drugs (owned by the pharmacy team), diagnoses (ICD-11 comes from the national list) or groups (a group needs an existing set concept; pick one with the group's pencil).

A8. Rules: when questions appear, warnings, calculations

Press a question's Rules button. The window Rules for "<question>" has up to four parts, each with a short help line. Press Save rules when done.

PartWhat you choose
When should this question appear?Always or Only when…, then one or more conditions: another question's answer (Any of these answers), whether it has an answer, a number comparison, Patient's age or Patient's sex. With several conditions choose All of them must be true or Any one of them is enough.
Warn below and Warn aboveA yellow warning so the clinician double-checks the value. The form can still be saved. Required is different: it is set in the pencil and blocks saving until answered.
Number formatAny number, Whole numbers only, Up to 1 decimal or Up to 2 decimals.
Fill in by itselfTyped in (not worked out), BMI from height (cm) and weight (kg), or Expected delivery date from the last period (LNMP). A worked-out value cannot be typed in.

Some older forms have rules too complex for these choices. The window then says This rule was written by hand. The rule keeps working and is left as it is, unless you press Replace it with a simple rule.

A9. The Check

The Check in the right-hand panel reads the whole form and lists problems in two groups.

GroupExamples
Must fix before exportA question with no concept; a concept or answer that does not exist on this EMR; two questions with the same ID; a rule naming a question that no longer exists; a drug form used as a question.
Worth a lookA retired concept; a group whose concept is not a set; a concept of an unusual class for a question; a concept still waiting to be created; a setting the Form Builder does not expect.

Press Check concepts on the server to confirm every concept exists in this EMR; when it finishes the panel says Concepts checked. Press Check again after you fix things. Downloading stays blocked, with the reason shown, until the identity is right, there are no errors, and the concepts have been checked. Of the 368 shipped forms, 357 pass with no errors.

A10. Export and hand over

When the Check is clean, press Download content files. You get <Form_name>-content.zip with a README.md for the reviewer, the form file in ampathforms/, and, if you created concepts, the rows to add to the concept list (master_new_concepts.APPEND.csv) and their number settings (zz_numeric_precision_fixes.APPEND.csv). Form JSON only downloads just the form file.

A concept the form uses that this EMR lacks shows as an error after Check concepts on the server; Create new concepts then proposes it with its existing code. Missing CIEL concepts are listed separately: ask the dictionary owner to load them. Form Studio never creates CIEL concepts.

  1. The files are added to the content repository on a form-updates branch, as the README says.
  2. Content checks and one real save test run on a test EMR.
  3. A pull request is reviewed and merged, and a new build brings the form to facilities.

B. The AI assistant

Part B: for clinicians

The assistant is a panel inside the patient chart with three tools: Help & Search, Form Filler and Diagnosis Assistant. Form Filler and Diagnosis Assistant also accept dictation through Speak. Answers come from an AI model on a separate AI service; the model's name (for example qwen2.5:14b) is shown under each answer.

What the assistant is not

Every answer ends with the same reminder: "AI-generated decision support. It may be inaccurate or incomplete and is not a substitute for clinical judgment. Verify everything against the patient and the record before acting." Form Filler never saves a form, and the Diagnosis Assistant never writes to the chart.

B1. Open the assistant

  1. Open a patient's chart.
  2. Press the round blue button at the bottom right, or press Alt+A. Press either again to close.
  3. Choose a tool under How can I help?.

The arrow at the top left of each tool goes back to this menu. The two-arrow button collapses the panel to a small bar.

KeysDoes
Alt+AOpen or close the assistant
Alt+Up or Alt+DownPrevious or next field (Form Filler)
Alt+ZUndo the last insert
First use on a computer

The AI service's security certificate is not yet trusted by browsers, so each user must accept a browser warning once. Until then Form Filler shows AI unavailable, used the rule-based extractor. Ask your administrator to open the AI service address with you once and accept the warning.

B2. Help & Search: ask a question and check the sources

  1. Choose Help & Search. The first time, it shows Loading forms… and Indexing forms n / 300 for a moment.
  2. Type in Search forms, fields, guides…, for example "How do I record vital signs for a patient?". Matching forms and fields appear at once, each tagged Form or Field; Open opens that form.
  3. Press Ask: answer from these results. An ANSWER box appears above the list.
  4. Read the answer. Each tag such as G2 or GL1 points at a source listed under Sources: (G = a form, GL = a guideline page).
  5. Check the sources before you act on the answer. A guideline source names the document and page, for example "WHO IMAI District Clinician Manual ... p. 229".
  6. Optional: press the thumbs up or down under Was this helpful?.

B3. Form Filler: fill a form from a note, then review

Form Filler works on a form that is open in the patient chart. Open one first: Clinical forms on the right of the chart, then the form's name. With no form open, Form Filler says No form open and offers Find a form.

One field at a time: This field

  1. Open the assistant and choose Form Filler. This field is selected and the first empty field is shown under FILLING, with its type and limits (for example Number, ≥ 0).
  2. Type the value, for example 120 or one twenty, and press Insert.
  3. Move with Previous and Next (or Alt+Up and Alt+Down). Undo takes back the last insert.

The whole form from one note: Whole form

  1. Choose Whole form. The panel shows the form's name and number of fields.
  2. Type the note under Type, or dictate it with Speak (B5). Example: "Blood pressure 130 over 85, pulse 96, temperature 38.2, respiratory rate 22, oxygen saturation 95 percent on room air. Weight 62 kg. Chief complaint fever and cough for 3 days."
  3. Press Find values (or Ctrl+Enter).
  4. Review each suggestion under FOUND N VALUES: the field, the value, a confidence percentage, and the exact words from your note it came from, in quotes.
  5. For each one, keep it (tick), change it (pencil) or drop it (X).
  6. Press Insert accepted (N). The values go into the form. Undo all takes them out again.
  7. Read the form yourself, complete what is missing, and press the form's own Save.

Below the suggestions, STILL EMPTY lists the fields the note did not fill, with the number that are required (marked *), and NOT MATCHED TO A FIELD shows parts of the note that fit no field. If the model proposes a value whose words are not in your note, Form Filler drops it and says so, for example "evidence not found in the text (ungrounded), dropped". When the AI service cannot be reached, a grey Rule-based label appears and a simpler extractor is used; check its suggestions with extra care.

B4. Diagnosis Assistant: possible diagnoses from the chart

  1. In the patient's chart, open the assistant and choose Diagnosis Assistant. The top line shows the patient (for example Female, 34 y) and how many chart records were loaded.
  2. Read the yellow line. "Not loaded: orders. The model will not see them." means orders are not sent today.
  3. Type the Presenting complaint, for example "fever and cough for 3 days".
  4. Add the History (optional) by typing or with Speak.
  5. Open Records to send: N to see each record the model will receive (age and sex, vital signs, encounters).
  6. Press Suggest differential.

Read the result

PartWhat it tells you
Yellow boxesLimited confidence and Some citations could not be verified list claims the assistant could not match to the patient's data. Treat those claims with care.
DIFFERENTIAL DIAGNOSISNumbered conditions, each marked Most likely or Consider with a likelihood. Lines marked with a plus support it; lines marked with a minus argue against it. Tags such as GL1 point at a guideline.

You decide

The list is a set of suggestions to think with. "Suggestions only: nothing is written to the chart." Diagnosis, tests and treatment remain the clinician's decision, recorded in the chart as usual.

B5. Speak: dictate in Amharic, English, or both

Speak turns speech into text in Form Filler (both views) and in the History box of the Diagnosis Assistant. The speech is processed on the same computer by a local speech service; the recording is not sent to the AI service.

  1. Choose Speak above the text box. A line starting Speech service ok confirms the speech service is running.
  2. Press the Speak button and allow the microphone when Chrome asks.
  3. Speak. The panel shows Listening… press Stop when done, a level meter and the time.
  4. Press Stop.
  5. The transcript appears in the box with Check the transcript below before inserting. Read it and correct any word by typing.
  6. Continue as usual: Find values in Form Filler, or Suggest differential.

Transcription only, no translation

Speak writes down what was said, in the language it was said. Amharic speech becomes Amharic text; it is not translated to English, and English speech is not translated to Amharic.

Clinicians often mix English medical words into Amharic. In Amharic mode two speech engines listen to the same recording. An English word is written in English only when the second engine is reasonably sure and the word is in the EMR's own medical vocabulary; otherwise it is written in Amharic letters (malaria becomes ማላሪያ). Check those words in the transcript.

Choose the language

Speak expects English unless it is set to Amharic. The setting is kept in your browser only, so set it once on each computer:

  1. Press the wrench icon at the top right (Implementer Tools). If you do not see it, ask your administrator.
  2. On Configuration, type asrLanguage in Search configuration.
  3. Open @palladium-ethiopia/esm-ai-assistant-app, press the pencil next to asrLanguage, type am and press Enter. (Type en to go back to English.)
  4. Close Implementer Tools.

Upload (marked SOON) next to Type and Speak is not available on this version yet.
